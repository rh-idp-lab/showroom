====
*Challenge #9: "Great, you built a platform. Now show me a developer can ship code without your help."*

The CTO is watching. You've built the platform, now prove it works. Switch perspective: you're no longer the Platform Engineer. You're a Golden Path Inc. developer who just needs to ship a feature. No Helm, no ArgoCD, no Vault, just the portal, a template, and your code.
====

Switch perspective: use the IDP you built as a developer would: from template selection to deployment.

== Run a software template

. Open https://backstage-rhdh.{openshift_cluster_ingress_domain}[Developer Hub^] and sign in.
. Go to **Create** and select a Software Template.
. Fill in the form: component name, team, description.
. Click **Create** and watch the execution.

== Observe the workflow

* **Repository created**: New repo appears in GitLab.
* **Pipeline triggered**: OpenShift Pipelines runs the build.
* **ArgoCD Application created**: In `rhdh-gitops` or your GitOps namespace.
* **Application deployed**: The app is deployed to the cluster.

== Make a code change

. Open https://devspaces.{openshift_cluster_ingress_domain}[Dev Spaces^].
. Open the workspace for your new component.
. Edit a file and push the change.
. Watch the pipeline re-run and the deployment update.

== Verify the signed artifact

If Trusted Artifact Signer is integrated with your pipeline, confirm that the built image is signed and verifiable.
